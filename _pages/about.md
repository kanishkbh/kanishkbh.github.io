I am a PhD student at Technical University of Munich at the [Thuerey group](https://ge.in.tum.de/), funded by the [Munich Center for Machine Learning](https://mcml.ai/). My research revolves around augmenting classic fluid simulation methods with machine learning. Currently I am working with hybrid models - neural networks that correct for numerical inaccuracies in legacy solvers.

Previously I have worked as an Application Specialist for Cloud HPC Workflows at the [Leibniz Supercomputing Centre, Munich](https://www.lrz.de/en/).
